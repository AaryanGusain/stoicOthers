Stoic Widgets

Privacy Policy

Effective November 24, 2026

Overview

Stoic Widgets is part of a family of related apps operated by WIDGET WORKS LLP, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, including Stoic Widgets, Dharma Widgets, and Buddhist Widgets. This policy explains what information the app handles, how that information is used, and what choices you have.

Stoic Widgets is designed to work offline first for core features. App analytics events and session recordings may be sent to PostHog even if you do not sign in.

Information We Handle

Depending on how you use the app, it may handle the following information:

  • display name
  • liked quotes
  • saved collections
  • followed topics
  • theme, wallpaper, and personalization preferences
  • reminder settings and streak data
  • widget configuration and widget preferences
  • subscription entitlement status needed to determine premium access
  • purchase email, web-funnel choices, and referral attribution when you use the web onboarding funnel
  • web subscription identifiers, billing status, renewal dates, and cancellation status
  • app interactions and session recordings, including typed text, screen images, and touch locations

Sign In And Account Data

You can use the app without creating an account.

If you choose Sign in with Apple, Apple may provide your name and email address as part of that sign-in flow. The app uses Firebase Authentication to create or sign in to your account.

If you sign in, eligible app data may be stored in Firebase Firestore so you can restore it on another device.

Subscriptions

App Store subscriptions, free trials, renewals, cancellations, and purchase restoration are handled through Apple and RevenueCat.

If you purchase annual or lifetime app access on the web, the payment provider or merchant of record identified at checkout handles payment authorization and payment-method information, and recurring charges for annual plans. This may be Paddle or Dodo Payments where offered; Razorpay handles existing Razorpay purchases. RevenueCat receives the purchase status and entitlement duration needed to make Stoic Widgets Pro available inside the app.

We do not receive or store your full payment card number. We store the purchase email, plan, payment and subscription identifiers, billing status, paid-through date, and account link needed to operate and support the subscription.

Web Funnel And Email

The web onboarding funnel stores your selected answers, an anonymous funnel session identifier, referral parameters, and the email address you provide. These details are used to build your result, complete checkout, send activation and management links, and understand where the funnel needs improvement.

Transactional emails include receipts, activation links, payment-status notices, recovery links, and cancellation confirmations. Product notes are sent only when you separately select the optional marketing consent control.

Analytics And Session Replay

We use PostHog and Vercel Analytics to understand web funnel screen views, selected actions, checkout outcomes, and technical performance. We also use PostHog in the Stoic Widgets app to understand app use and improve onboarding, paywalls, and reliability.

We record app onboarding and paywall visits and a sample of other app sessions. These recordings may be linked to an app session, device, or signed-in account identifier. They can include screen images, touch locations, text you type into app fields (including your name, email address, searches, and one-time sign-in codes), visible images and system views, console logs, and network activity metadata such as request URLs and status. Some operating-system or third-party screens may not be available to the recorder. We may use AI-assisted analysis of recordings to find where people hesitate or get stuck.

Web funnel event analytics and app session recordings are different data sources. App recordings can include an email address you type even when a web funnel event does not include it.

Reflect with Marc

Reflect with Marc offers guided Stoic passages, explanations, and small practices that work on your device. You can also write one message a day to receive a personal reply.

The first time you write to Marc, the app asks for your permission in the chat before anything is sent. With your permission, the message you write is sent through our server (Firebase) to Anthropic, which generates the reply. Anthropic normally deletes API inputs and outputs within 30 days, with exceptions for safety and legal obligations. We do not store the text of your messages or replies on our servers; we keep only usage counters needed to limit requests.

Your conversation is saved on your device for up to 24 hours. Messages you write to Marc are excluded from session recordings. You can turn personal replies off, clear the conversation, or report a reply in Profile → Privacy & Security.

Permissions And Device Features

The app may request access to certain device features only when needed for the feature you choose to use.

  • Notifications, to send quote reminders and streak reminders if you enable them
  • Photos, to save exported quote wallpapers or quote images to your library
  • System share and clipboard features, when you explicitly choose to share or copy quote content

How Information Is Used

  • operate the app and its widgets
  • save your preferences and personalization choices
  • restore data across devices if you sign in
  • schedule and manage reminders you enable
  • determine premium access and subscription status
  • respond to support or privacy requests
  • analyze app use and recordings to improve onboarding, paywalls, and reliability

Local Storage And Optional Sync

The app stores data locally on your device first. If you sign in, some app data may also sync to Firebase Firestore, including items such as your display name, liked quotes, collections, followed topics, theme selections, streak data, and reminder preferences.

Some data remains device-local and is not synced to Firestore.

  • widget configurations stored for the device widget extension
  • device-specific notification scheduling identifiers
  • other device-local operational state used only on that device

Sharing With Service Providers

We do not sell your personal data.

Information is shared only as needed with service providers that support the features you choose to use.

  • Apple, for Sign in with Apple, App Store billing, subscriptions, and platform services
  • RevenueCat, for subscription and entitlement management
  • Firebase, for authentication and optional cloud sync
  • The provider shown at checkout (Paddle, Dodo Payments, or Razorpay), for payment processing, tax and billing administration, refunds, and fraud prevention
  • Resend, for transactional and consented product email
  • PostHog, for web and app analytics, session recordings, and replay analysis; Vercel, for web analytics, hosting, and technical operation of the web funnel
  • Anthropic, to generate the personal replies you choose to request in Reflect with Marc

Retention And Deletion

Locally stored app data remains on your device until you remove it, delete the app, or use the app's delete-data controls.

If you are signed in, synced account data may remain in Firebase until you delete your account through the app's account deletion flow, subject to platform reauthentication requirements.

Subscription and billing records may be maintained by Apple, the applicable web payment provider, RevenueCat, and us for the period needed to provide access, meet accounting or legal obligations, resolve disputes, and prevent fraud.

PostHog retains app analytics and recordings under the project's retention settings. Deleting local app data or your account does not automatically erase recordings already sent to PostHog. Contact us to request access to or deletion of this data.

Your Choices

  • use the app without creating an account
  • choose whether to sign in
  • choose whether to enable reminders
  • choose whether to grant photo-library access
  • delete local app data from the device
  • delete synced account data through the app if signed in
  • disable notifications at any time in iOS settings
  • contact us to request access to or deletion of analytics and session recordings
  • turn off personal replies from Marc, or clear your conversation with Marc, in Profile → Privacy & Security

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the effective date above will be updated. Your continued use of the app after an updated policy becomes effective means the updated policy will apply going forward.
