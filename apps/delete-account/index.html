Last Updated: May 28, 2026

How to Delete Your Account In-App

If you have created an account (e.g., via Sign in with Apple) and wish to delete your account and all synced data, you can do so directly from within the Stoic Widgets app:

  • Open the Stoic Widgets app on your device.
  • Navigate to your Profile page.
  • Tap on Privacy & Security.
  • Select the Delete Account option and confirm your choice.

Once confirmed, your account and all associated cloud-synced data (including display name, liked quotes, and personalized preferences stored in Firebase) will be immediately and permanently deleted from our servers.

How to Delete Device-Local Data

If you use Stoic Widgets without an account (offline mode), your data is stored solely on your device. To delete this data, simply remove the app from your device:

  • Press and hold the Stoic Widgets app icon on your home screen.
  • Select Remove App (or Delete App).
  • Confirm to delete the app and all its local storage.

Request Deletion via Email

If you no longer have the app installed, or if you prefer us to manually delete your account and all associated user data, you can submit a request via email. We will process your deletion request within 7 business days.

Click the button below to draft a deletion request email from your email client:

Request Account Deletion

Alternatively, you can manually send an email to stoicismwidget@yahoo.com with the subject line "Stoic Widgets - Account Deletion Request" and include your registered email address.
